Google Business Profile sync: what switches it on
(read against developers.google.com/my-business on 2026-10-22)

The Gold tab's Google card works without any of this: it shows the kit (hours, description,
phone, link, offers, each with a copy button) and a link to business.google.com. The
Connect button appears once the three secrets below exist.

1. Google account and Cloud project
   - One Google account that owns or manages a verified Business Profile with a website,
     live for 60 days or more. It is the account the request is made from.
   - console.cloud.google.com: create a project (for example "Mot Dang Pages"). Note its
     Project Number (Project info card on the dashboard).

2. Ask Google for Business Profile API access (the quota is 0 until this is approved)
   - Form: https://support.google.com/business/contact/api_default
     choose "Application for Basic API Access". Use the email that is owner or manager
     of the profile. Give the Project Number, the business website, and the reason:
     "sites.motdang.net makes web pages for shop owners; with each owner's consent it
     copies the page's hours, description, phone, link, photos and offers to the owner's
     own profile."
   - Approved when APIs & Services > Quotas shows 300 requests per minute for the
     Business Profile APIs (0 means not approved).

3. Enable these APIs in the project (API Library, each one, Enable)
   - My Business Account Management API
   - My Business Business Information API
   - Google My Business API (v4; appears after approval; localPosts and media use it)
   (The Lodging, Place Actions, Notifications and Verifications APIs are not used.)

4. OAuth consent screen (APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen)
   - User type External. App name: Mot Dang Pages. Support email and developer email.
   - App home page: https://sites.motdang.net
   - Authorised domain: motdang.net
   - Privacy policy link: a page on motdang.net that says what is read and sent (Google
     asks for one for a sensitive scope).
   - Scope to add: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage
   - While the app is in Testing, only listed test users can connect (up to 100) and
     their sign-in lapses after 7 days. Press Publish app to remove both limits; Google
     may then ask to verify the app for the scope.

5. OAuth client (APIs & Services > Credentials > Create credentials > OAuth client ID)
   - Application type: Web application. Name: Mot Dang Pages.
   - Authorised JavaScript origins: none needed.
   - Authorised redirect URI, exactly:  https://sites.motdang.net/api/gbp/callback
   - Copy the client ID and client secret.

6. Secrets on the Worker (not committed anywhere)
   npx wrangler secret put GBP_CLIENT_ID
   npx wrangler secret put GBP_CLIENT_SECRET
   npx wrangler secret put GBP_KEY        (any long random string; seals refresh tokens in D1)
   npx wrangler d1 migrations apply motdang-pages --remote    (0030, 0031) before deploying

Then, on a Gold page's Gold tab: Connect Google Business Profile, sign in, pick the
business, Send now.

Not yet confirmed against a live Google account: the 24-hour form of regularHours
(closeTime hours 24), whether Google accepts the WebP photo files the pages store (a
refused photo is named on the card and not asked again), and offer posts with a start
date later than today.
