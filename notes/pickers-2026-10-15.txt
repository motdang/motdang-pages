THE BUILD

Kinds
1. All nine on screen on a phone: a 3-column grid, no side scroll.
2. Each tile carries its line (Menu · GrabFood · map).
3. Under the grid, "Your page gets": the parts of that kind's page as
   chips. Tap one and the phone scrolls to that part and lights it.
4. Hover lifts a tile; only the picked tile has the red ring and tick.

Looks

   It retypes as the name is typed.

7. Colour dots under the looks. The page takes the colour now, not later.
8. Arrows on the phone (‹ Night market ›) and a swipe on the phone change
   the look in place. On a computer, pointing at a look shows it on the phone.
