BUILT

A new kind in the builder: ร้านพระเครื่อง / Amulet seller / 佛牌店.
Each amulet: name, temple, monk or maker, year (BE or CE; Thai readers see พ.ศ., others see both eras), kind (tablet, coin, cast figure, takrut, locket, yant cloth, charm, altar Buddha), material (15 from amulet-essentials), mould, size, condition (สวยแชมป์ to หัก), frame, certificate, price or none ("สอบถามราคา"), status, notes, up to 12 photos.
Status words follow web-pra: พร้อมเช่า, จองแล้ว, เช่าแล้ว, พระโชว์. Price and the reserve button hide on เช่าแล้ว and พระโชว์.
Each amulet has its own page, /name/a/<id>: front and back side by side, tap to zoom, the facts, the price, the seller's own guarantee and delivery words, the other amulets, a message form.
LINE reserve: with a LINE Official Account (@id) the button opens LINE with "สนใจเช่าบูชา <name> <link>" written. With a personal LINE id it copies that message, then opens LINE.
The page: a grid two across on a phone, filters for available and for each kind.
The builder on a phone: one fold per amulet, a Take photos button that puts photos straight into that amulet (front first), Fix (the picture studio) on each photo, a tap to change status.
Search engines and link previews: each amulet page has its own title, description and picture (the front photo), and is in the sitemap on Gold pages.
Labels in Thai, English and Chinese; English fills the other nine page languages.

HOW SELLERS SELL NOW (public sources)

Facebook groups and pages carry most of the trade; the largest group had 164,462 members (thaipublica, 2018). Thairath (June 2026) adds live streams and shopping apps, and lists the scams: a genuine photo then a fake sent, fake pages copying known dealers, payment to an account that is not the seller's.
web-pra.com shops: free for 30 amulets for 6 months; ฿2,000 a year for 150, ฿3,000 for 300, ฿4,000 for 500, ฿5,000 for 1,000, ฿7,000 for 2,000. Its rule: every listing guaranteed genuine for life.
Prices run from ฿39 on Lazada to millions; a web-pra shop sampled sat at ฿300–1,200.

THE AMULET CAMERA (Gold, 2026-10-16)

At the top of the amulets in the builder: Amulet camera, and Or pick photos. Each photo becomes a new amulet at the
top of the page, named "พระองค์ที่ 7" until the seller names it, its front photo cut out and laid on white.
The photo is kept in the phone's browser (IndexedDB) the moment it is taken and let go only once it is on the page;
a crash, a closed tab or no signal loses nothing, and reopening the builder finishes it.
The cutting runs in a frame from Amulet Camera (nanobotco.github.io/amulet-camera/cut.html): ISNet in the phone's
browser, about 45 MB the first time. The builder's own security rules allow that frame and nothing else of it.
Each amulet with a photo has Search (Google Lens on its front photo, once the photo has passed its check) and
Post (the share sheet with the photo; the caption with its name, price and page link is copied for a Facebook group).
Free pages see one line: Gold, the amulet camera. Every new page is Gold for its first 30 days.
Tested 2026-10-16 headless at phone size: two photos became two amulets in 47 s (the first cut includes the model
download); a photo whose tab was closed mid-way came back and finished. Code: public/s/make-amcam.mjs.
