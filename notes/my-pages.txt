What a page carries
- Its place drawn by Mot Dang's auto-doodler from the page's name, kind and hours, lit by the sun where the pin is; the maker picks the scene (noodle shop, café, massage, market …). A tap turns it to morning, evening, night.
- The drawn map: the doodle-map engine on the world tiles, any country, the pin in the middle; in the north it draws Mot Dang's places around it. A tap opens it to the whole screen.
- Moving dingbats beside the name (the maker picks up to six) and beside each heading.
- LINE add, LINE QR, order from the menu on LINE, LINE MAN, GrabFood, Grab ride in Grab's 8 countries, Google and Apple directions, how far, hours with open now, event countdown and calendar file, save contact, printable A4 sign.
- A message form. Messages wait in the builder's Messages tab; a copy goes by email when the maker confirms an address (25 a page a day, 90 in all, from pages@motdang.net through Resend).
- Share to LINE, Facebook, WhatsApp, X, Telegram, email; a QR to save; a 1080×1920 picture for a story; a share card.
- Free pictures: public domain and CC0 from Wikimedia Commons, the Cleveland Museum of Art and Openverse. The credit is read again at the source and printed under the picture and in a Pictures list.

The builder's tabs: Page, Messages, Traffic (people a day, visits, button taps, where people came from, countries, devices, hours; machines counted apart), Gold.
